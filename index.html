About YUVA SETU

Bridging Skills and Opportunities

YUVA SETU is designed to create a common digital bridge between students, educational institutes, industry requirements and government.

Students can understand how closely their skills match specific job roles. Institutes can see skills currently demanded by industries, while the Government/Admin dashboard can monitor all three data sources centrally.

🎓 Student Alignment

Student skills are compared with Government-managed industry requirements.

🏢 Industry Requirements

Job roles and required skills are maintained centrally by Government/Admin.

🏫 Institute Monitoring

Institutes update the skills they teach and view changing industry demand.
