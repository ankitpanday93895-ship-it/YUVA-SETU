About YUVA SETU

Connecting education, skills and employment requirements.

Bridging Skills and Opportunities

YUVA SETU creates a common digital bridge between students, educational institutes, industry requirements and Government/Admin.

Students can compare their current skills with job-role requirements. Institutes can understand which skills are currently demanded by industries. Government/Admin can monitor student, industry and institute data from one dashboard.

🎓 Student Skill Alignment

Identify matched, missing and additional skills for selected job roles.

🏢 Industry Requirements

Government/Admin manages job roles and required skill data.

🏫 Institute Skill Monitoring

Institutes update skills being taught and view industry demand.
